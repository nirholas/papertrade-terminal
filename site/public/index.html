Quickstart

1. Open it

Open the terminal, connect any EIP-6963 wallet and switch to HyperEVM. Or paste an address to watch read-only.

2. Enable trading

Sign one gas-free message to register a session key. Fund the account from the Fund tab.

3. Trade

Pick a market, side, margin and leverage, read the ladder, review and confirm. Prefill any of it with ?market=BTC&side=long&margin=1000&leverage=10.

Open in Papertrade OSRun the terminal as a window in the Papertrade desktop.
